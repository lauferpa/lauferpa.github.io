01

THE FOUNDATION • MOTOROLA / FIRST DATA

I learned to build before I learned to market.

My early career was grounded in project administration, change control, product lifecycle work and resource management. At Motorola, I managed concurrent iDEN schedules and helped lead change-control governance across teams. Later, in the Messaging Division, the work expanded into product roadmaps, lifecycle planning, end-of-life decisions and replacement strategy.

That foundation taught me something I still use today: great marketing execution depends on understanding dependencies, timing, stakeholders and what has to happen before the market ever sees the launch.

Foundation built

Project Management • Change Control • Product Lifecycle • Roadmaps • Training • Cross-Functional Coordination

02

GO-TO-MARKET • MOTOROLA / BLACKBERRY

Then the work moved into the market.

Working with Sprint at Motorola moved me closer to the customer and the commercial engine: launches, retail, field, digital, partner activity, enablement, promotions and B2B/B2C execution. Public-safety initiatives added another dimension, translating rugged technology into real-world use for police and crisis-response teams.

At BlackBerry, the canvas became Latin America. Marketing operations, enterprise mobility, secure communications, government programs and regional execution came together at much greater scale.

From execution to infrastructure

50+ LATAM vendors • regional RFP leadership • marketing and sales materials infrastructure • enterprise and government programs • scalable processes and tools

03

REGIONAL LEADERSHIP • SAMSUNG

The market became a stage.

At Samsung, regional GTM stretched across mobile, consumer electronics and enterprise solutions. The work was deliberately 360°: campaigns, retail, carriers, distributors, channel programs, launches, PR, media, events and public-sector initiatives, backed by a $1M+ quarterly regional budget.

04

DIGITAL EVOLUTION • IK MULTIMEDIA → VMWARE / OMNISSA

Digital became a growth engine.

What began with building audiences and localized social content evolved into integrated digital demand: paid media, partner campaigns, webinars, content, activation and measurable business outcomes.

At IK Multimedia, I built the Spanish-language social presence from the ground up while supporting global hardware and software launches across North America, LATAM and EMEA. Localized content, organic and paid social, product storytelling and performance reporting helped create a measurable market presence, producing 300% follower growth in the first months.

Later, through VMware and Omnissa partner marketing, those digital capabilities expanded into B2B demand programs: creating digital content, organizing webinars, running LinkedIn and other digital advertising, supporting co-branded partner campaigns and connecting engagement to partner activation, leads, pipeline and ROI.

05

ECOSYSTEM SCALE • OMNISSA

The ecosystem became the strategy.

At Omnissa, the work moved deeply into SaaS partner ecosystems. I owned Americas partner marketing across Workspace ONE and Horizon while supporting global enablement and campaign programs. Strategy now had to work through partners, distributors, alliances, Sales and public-sector priorities at the same time.

That meant joint GTM planning, MDF strategy, onboarding, enablement, campaigns, webinars, events, reporting and governance, with an approximately $800K quarterly Americas partner budget.

TODAY • GITHUB • LATAM

All of those chapters converge here.

Today, I bring that full career arc into LATAM marketing at GitHub: regional strategy, Revenue collaboration, technical audiences, webinars, in-person activations and market development.

The technology is different. The audience is increasingly technical. The opportunity is enormous. But the job still comes back to the same craft: connect people, market needs and technology in a way that creates momentum.
